### Post 1 — legendaryjman, 2026-08-15

The goal with every armor set is to get to the caps in Damage Resistance and Defense, but where do they start?

I got curious as to what is the baseline Damage Resistance and Defense numbers for each of the armor sets. I went into CoH Sidekick and looked at each of them and was surprised at how wildly different they really are. I decided to put the numbers here in case anyone else is also curious.

Here is what I did: I went into each powerset on the Tanker AT (numbers will be the same ratio, but smaller on all the other ATs), I took all of the powers that gave Damage Resistance or Defense. I 3-slotted them with level 55 Dam/Def IOs. I also gave each power the best possible scenario. If a power gave a bonus from each enemy near it I gave it the highest enemy count.
