[Archetype Proposal] The Tactician — Assault Primary / Buff-Debuff Secondary

The "Blapper-Style" Frontline Combat Support

**High Concept & Pitch:**

In comic books, some of the most iconic heroes don't hang back on the periphery. Characters like **Captain America**, **Black Canary**, **Gambit**, or an internal martial artist like **Iron Fist** lead directly from the front rank: they weave fluidly between physical strikes and ranged concussive blasts, but their true superpower is **tactical force multiplication**—staggering enemy formations with soft crowd control while coordinating, bolstering, and elevating their squad.

In City of Heroes, support powersets have steadily evolved toward close-quarters, point-blank gameplay. However, traditional Defenders and Corruptors have purely ranged primaries with zero native melee attacks until late-game Epic pools.

**The Tactician** bridges this gap by introducing a dedicated **"blapper-style" combat support archetype**. Pairing an **Assault Primary** with a **Buff/Debuff Secondary**, Tacticians combine active skirmishing with team force-multiplication. Rather than relying on passive armor shields, they survive through active tactical disruption: flooring enemy accuracy, stacking debuffs, and escalating soft crowd control (knockdowns, stumbles, and stuns).

The Evolution of Support: Built for the Front Line

The classic pushback against melee support concepts is: "Without toggled armor shields or mez protection, you will fold immediately in melee."

However, modern Homecoming support set design has moved far beyond passive backline clicking. Several premier sets feature mobile auras, built-in status effect resistance, and point-blank engagement tools that actively reward being inside the brawl:

-

**Light Affinity:** Lightfield is a mobile PBAoE toggle granting continuous refreshing Absorb alongside team resistance to Holds, Immobilizes, and Disorients. Paired with Radiance (-DMG/-ToHit to nearby foes) and Prismatic Shield, the set provides self-contained, mobile frontline protection.

-

**Marine Affinity:** Features built-in battlefield mobility with Whitecap (offensive damage, knockdown, -Res, and self-teleport) and Toroidal Bubble (PBAoE team endurance/recovery and damage resistance). You don't hang back—you crash into the pack like a wave.

-

**Electrical Affinity:** Faraday Cage grants an anchored fortress of team damage resistance, knockback protection, and status effect (mez) protection right where the combat is happening.

-

**Force Field (Revamped):** Dispersion Bubble provides a mobile PBAoE team defense shield with built-in Hold/Immob/Stun protection, while Repulsion Field and Force Bomb actively disrupt adjacent mobs.

-

**Classic Frontline Sets (Time, Kinetics, Storm):** Sets like Time Manipulation (Time's Juncture PBAoE -ToHit aura), Storm Summoning (Hurricane), and Kinetics (Fulcrum Shift / Transference) have always delivered their highest value at point-blank range.

The support tools to survive and thrive in melee already exist in the game. The Tactician simply provides the native melee combat attacks to back them up.

Inherent Mechanic: Tactical Cadence

Because Tacticians lack passive armor toggles, survivability is earned directly through proactive engagement:

-

**Building Cadence:** Activating attacks from your Assault primary charges the **Tactical Cadence** meter. Melee-range attacks generate significantly more Cadence than ranged attacks, rewarding players for diving into the thick of combat.

-

**Soft-Control Magnification:** As Cadence increases, the secondary crowd-control effects native to your Assault attacks are amplified. Minor knockdown chances scale up toward reliable knockdowns, and Disorients/Stuns gain bonus magnitude and duration to help check high-rank lieutenants and bosses.

-

**Combat Flow:**

-

Open with ranged debuffs or a closing strike.

-

Engage in melee to build Cadence and knock down priority targets.

-

Deploy high-impact point-blank support powers while enemies are disrupted.

-

Weave ranged attacks and reposition as the encounter demands.

Frequently Addressed Questions

**Q: Why not just play a Corruptor or Defender with an Epic melee pool?**

A: Epic pools grant 1–2 melee attacks at level 35+. The Tactician offers a cohesive melee/ranged hybrid rotation right from Level 1, supporting a fluid blapper playstyle throughout the entire leveling curve.

**Q: Does this make Dominators or Corruptors obsolete?**

A: No. Dominators retain primary hard lockdowns (Mass Holds, Cages, Sleeps) and Domination. Corruptors retain heavy ranged alpha strikes and Scourge executes. The Tactician fills the frontline combat coordinator role.
